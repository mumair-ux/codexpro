SAFE BY DESIGN

Useful checks, without store access.

CodexPro reads your exported CSV only in your browser. It does not upload customer or product data, connect to Shopify, or update inventory. Some checks are heuristic: shared titles or images may be intentional, and a product CSV cannot verify collection membership, visual image similarity or live storefront styling. Inventory CSVs are validated separately for location, variant identity and stock fields.
